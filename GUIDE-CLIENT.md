# How to change your website

You can change your website from your phone. No code. You cannot break the design.

**You need:** your website address + `/admin` (example: `yourshop.com/admin`) and your password. Atelier Penh sends them to you on WhatsApp.

---

## 1. Open the editor

Open **yourshop.com/admin** on your phone.
Type your password. Tap **Log in**.

> Tip: add this page to your home screen. Then it opens like an app.
> To see the editor in French, tap **FR** at the top.

## 2. Choose what to change

Tap a big button:

| Button | What you can change |
|---|---|
| ✏️ **Texts** | Name, slogan, "About us", main photo |
| 🍽️ **Menu & prices** | Dishes, drinks, services, prices |
| 📷 **Photos** | Add, change or remove photos |
| 🕒 **Opening hours** | Open or Closed, times for each day |
| 📞 **Contact** | Phone, WhatsApp, address, map link |
| 💬 **Social media** | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok links |

## 3. Make your change

- **Text or price:** tap the box and type.
- **Photo:** tap **Choose a photo**. Take a picture or pick one from your phone.
- **Menu item:** tap its name to open it. Use **+ Add an item**, **✕ Remove**, **↑ Move up**.
- **Day closed:** tap **Closed**.

Your changes are saved on your phone. Nobody sees them yet.

## 4. Check with Preview

Tap **👁 Preview** at the bottom.
You see your website with your changes. Scroll to check.
Something wrong? Tap **Back to editing**.

## 5. Publish

Tap the green **✓ Publish** button. Tap **Yes**.
Wait 1 or 2 minutes. Your website is updated for everyone. ✅

---

### Problems?

- **"Wrong password"**: check it and try again. After 10 wrong tries, wait 15 minutes.
- **"Changed somewhere else"**: tap OK. The latest version loads. Make your change again.
- **Want to start again?** Tap **Cancel my changes** on the home screen.
- **Stuck?** Send a WhatsApp message to Atelier Penh with a screenshot.
