FAQ

The questions shop owners ask me.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. I build it before we meet. If it is not for you, you owe nothing and I do not publish it.

How do I pay?

50% when you say yes and 50% when the site is live, by ABA or KHQR. Delivery times start when I have your photos and texts. Extra changes beyond the 3 included in the first year are $35 each.

What happens after the first year?

The yearly follow-up keeps your domain, hosting, Google listing and 3 changes a year: $99, $149 or $199 a year depending on your plan, $299 a year for hotels and resorts.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and the code is handed over if you ever move to someone else.

Can I have a .com.kh address?

Yes, if your business is registered with the Ministry of Commerce: $100 extra to set it up, then $30 a year.

How do bookings reach me?

The booking form opens WhatsApp or Telegram with the message already written, straight to your number.

Which languages can the site be in?

English and French, each with its own address so Google shows the right one. Khmer, Japanese and Chinese are not offered yet: they will come once I have native proofreaders.

Can I change a photo myself?

Yes, for free, with your own editor. Or send it to me on WhatsApp: it is online the same day or the next, and 3 changes are included in the first year.

Do you use AI?

Yes, among other modern tools, so I can work faster and charge less than an agency. The art direction, the texts and every choice on your site are made by me, for your business.
