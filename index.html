FAQ

The questions shop owners ask me.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. I build it before we meet. If it is not for you, you owe nothing and I do not publish it.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and the code is handed over if you ever move to someone else.

How do bookings reach me?

The booking form opens WhatsApp or Telegram with the message already written, straight to your number. Premium adds automatic confirmations, reminders and online deposits.

Can the site be in Khmer or French?

Yes. Signature includes one extra language, Premium two. Each language gets its own address so Google shows the right one.

Can I change a photo myself?

Send it to me on WhatsApp and it is online the same day or the next. If you prefer to edit texts and photos yourself, I can add a simple editor.

Do you use AI?

Yes, among other modern tools, so I can work faster and charge less than an agency. The art direction, the texts and every choice on your site are made by me, for your business.
