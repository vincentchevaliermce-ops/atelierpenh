Privacy

Your data, in plain words.

This is a small website run by one person. Here is everything it does with your data, and everything it does not do.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who runs this site

Atelier Penh is me, Vincent, a French web designer based in Phnom Penh. For any question about this page or your data, write to me at vincent@atelierpenh.com.

No account, no ad cookies

You do not need an account to use this site. There are no advertising cookies, no ad trackers and no social media pixels.

Settings saved on your device

Your browser remembers a few choices on your own device: sound on or off, light or 3D mode, whether you have already seen the opening screen. They never leave your device and are never sent to me.

Visitor statistics

This site does not use any visitor statistics at the moment.

The shop name tool and the contact form

Nothing you type on this site is sent to a server. The “And your shop?” tool and the contact form only prepare a message. When you tap WhatsApp, Telegram or Email, that app opens with the message ready, and you decide whether to send it. On Telegram, the message is copied so you can paste it. From then on, your message goes through that app and follows its own privacy rules.

When you write to me

I use your message to answer you and to prepare your preview. If you want me to delete what you sent me, just ask.

Fonts, images and videos

Fonts, images, videos and scripts are served by this site itself, so your browser does not contact other companies to show the page. The only outside links are the ones you tap: WhatsApp, Telegram, email and the Moana Beauty Spa website.

Credits

Sound effects: CC0 (public domain) recordings from freesound.org. The previews show design concepts I made for local shops from their own photos; those photos belong to the shops. The 3D street was built for this site.
