What your restaurant site does

A menu people can read

A real web page, not a blurry PDF or a photo of the menu. Prices, dishes and allergens stay sharp on any phone, and I update it when your menu changes.

Table requests to your phone

Guests pick a date, a time and the number of people. The request opens in WhatsApp or Telegram with everything written. No booking app, no commission.

Found on Google Maps

Address, opening hours and a direct link from your Google listing to your site. Tourists and expats search there first.

In the languages of your guests

English by default, with French if you want. Each language gets its own page, so Google can show the right one.

Your photos, your style

I start from your logo, your colours and your room. The site looks like your restaurant, not like a template.

Keep your tools

Already on a booking platform or a delivery app? Your site links to it. You keep what works.

Questions owners ask

Can I change the menu myself?

Most owners prefer to send me a photo or a message, and I update it. Changes are included in the monthly fee: one per quarter on Essential, one per month on Signature, two per month on Premium.

Do guests book a precise time?

Yes. They choose a day, a time and the number of people, and the request arrives on your WhatsApp or Telegram. You confirm in one reply.

Do you take a commission on bookings?

No. You pay the setup and the monthly fee. Bookings that come through your site cost you nothing more.

I already use a booking tool. Do I have to drop it?

No. Your site can link to it. Some restaurants keep their tool for groups and use WhatsApp for everything else.

Want to see your own site first?

Send me your Facebook page, Instagram or Google Maps link. I design a free preview from your brand and show it to you, in your shop or on WhatsApp. You pay only if you say yes.
