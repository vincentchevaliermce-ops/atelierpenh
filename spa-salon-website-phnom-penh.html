What your spa or salon site does

A page per treatment

Massage, facial, nails, lashes, hair: each treatment has its own page with duration and price. Clients choose before they write, and Google can show each page.

Booking requests by treatment and time

The client picks a treatment, a day and a time slot. The request opens in WhatsApp or Telegram, ready to send. You confirm in one reply.

Prices that build trust

Clear prices remove the first question and the fear of a surprise bill. Clients who arrive already know what they booked.

Calm, fast, on any phone

Soft design that matches your space, pages light enough for 4G, and nothing that pops up in the client's face.

Reviews and Google Maps

Your Google rating and your location linked to the site, so a client who searches "spa near me" finds you and trusts you.

Optional deposit

On Premium, clients can leave a deposit online through ABA PayWay to reduce no-shows.

Moana Beauty Spa is a beauty spa in Tonle Bassac, a short walk from BKK1. Each treatment has its own page, prices are clear before anyone walks in, and every booking request lands in the team's messages.

Questions owners ask

Can clients book a specific therapist?

Yes, if you want it. The booking form can ask for a therapist, a treatment and a time slot, and everything arrives in one message.

Do I need booking software?

No. Requests arrive on WhatsApp or Telegram, where your team already answers clients. If you outgrow that, we can connect a booking tool later.

Can I change prices myself?

Send me the new price list and I update it. Changes are included in the monthly fee.
