Why schools choose it

Set in Cambodia

Rivers, markets, tuk-tuks and rice fields. Children read about a world they know, so they want to read more.

At the right level

Three levels, Lab 1 to Lab 3, from Pre-A1 to A2. Vocabulary built on the Cambridge Young Learners word lists.

Built for listening

A clear, natural voice at two speeds, and listening questions in every quiz. Every child hears good English, even in a class of 25.

Inside every story

A real page from Dara and the Ten Mangoes. Press play, or tap the word "mangoes".

Every Saturday, Dara helps Grandma. Grandma sells at the market. Today, Grandma has ten yellow mangoes in a big basket.
