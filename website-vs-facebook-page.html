Website or Facebook page: what does a Phnom Penh business need?

What a Facebook page does well, where it falls short, what Google says about local ranking, and the simple setup that uses both.

Guide · Updated 7 October 2026 · 5 min read

Many businesses in Phnom Penh run everything from a Facebook page, and it works up to a point. The question is what you lose by having nothing else. Here is an honest comparison, with no invented statistics.

What a Facebook page does well

  • It is free and quick to set up.
  • Your regular customers already follow you there.
  • Messenger makes it easy to answer questions.
  • Photos and daily posts show that the business is alive.

Where a Facebook page falls short

  • You rent, you do not own. Your page lives on someone else's platform, with their rules and their layout. A domain name and a website are yours.
  • Information gets buried. Prices, menu or opening hours posted three months ago are hard to find under newer posts. A website keeps them in one stable place.
  • Visitors from abroad search Google first. Tourists and new expats look on Google and Google Maps before they arrive. A clear website gives Google something precise to show.
  • Every question becomes a message. "How much?" and "Are you open?" take your time. A page with prices and hours answers them before they are asked.

What Google says about local ranking

Google explains that local results depend on three things: relevance, distance and prominence. On prominence, Google writes that it includes "how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have". A website gives your Business Profile a destination, gives other sites something to link to, and gives each of your services its own page that can match a search.

The best answer: both, linked

  1. Keep your Facebook page and Instagram for daily life and community.
  2. Put the stable information on your website: prices, menu or treatments, hours, address, booking.
  3. Link the website from your Facebook page, your Instagram bio and your Google Business Profile.
  4. Send booking requests from the website straight to WhatsApp or Telegram, where you already answer.

When a Facebook page is enough

If almost all your customers are regulars who already know you, and you do not want more visitors from outside, a well-kept Facebook page can be enough for now. A website starts to pay when you want people who do not know you yet to find you, trust you and book.
